How a pack works

Every class video, in the group chat, and by next week nobody can find the move. A pack fixes that in one link.

  1. 1

    Mark the class's moves

    Bookmark the moves in your class videos, in the order you want them learnt, and put them in a pack.

  2. 2

    Send one invite

    Share the invite link in your class group. Students tap it, join, and the moves arrive in their library.

  3. 3

    Add to it every week

    New moves reach everyone in the pack. Review each update before it goes out, or let updates go automatically.
