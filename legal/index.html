What data we collect

Clipento is designed with privacy at its core. The app works entirely on your device without requiring an account. However, if you choose to enable cloud sync to back up your bookmarks and data across devices, you'll create an optional account using your email address.

We may collect the following to keep the app running smoothly:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • Crash reports and performance data (via Google Crashlytics)
  • Anonymous usage analytics (via PostHog) — only if you allow it when the app asks; you can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy
  • Email address and display name — only if you sign in, which you do to use cloud sync or to open a Teaching Pack
  • User account identifier (Firebase Auth UID) — only if you sign in, used to recognise your account across devices and packs
  • Video location metadata (latitude and longitude) — captured when your device records videos and stored with the video on your device. If you use cloud sync, it is also stored with the video's details in your cloud library. It is never shared with your students.
  • Photos you choose for the Programme Scanner, and a cover image you add to a Teaching Pack — only if you use cloud sync or publish a pack. They are stored in Google Firebase Storage so they are available on your devices and, for a pack cover, to your students.
  • Links to YouTube videos you add, with details such as the video's title and thumbnail — see YouTube videos

What data we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name unless you sign in. If you do, we store the display name you choose and your email address
  • We do not access your microphone. We use your camera only when you choose to photograph a timetable for the Programme Scanner
  • We do not upload your videos
  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not use your data for advertising or marketing
  • Bookmarks, tags, collections and notes you create stay on your device by default — we never see them unless you enable cloud sync

Analytics & your choice

We use PostHog (EU-hosted, GDPR-compliant) to collect anonymous usage analytics, if you allow it. This helps us understand which features are used most and how you interact with Clipento, so we can make it better. Analytics data is pseudonymous — it never includes your videos, bookmarks, tags, notes, or any personal content you create.

How analytics works

We generate a stable, anonymous device identifier (UUID) and store it on your device. This UUID is sent to PostHog with each analytics event to track your usage patterns across sessions. The UUID is never linked to your email, account, identity, or personal data. You can copy this identifier from Settings → Privacy → Copy Analytics ID. If you want to reset it and generate a new one for future analytics, use Settings → Privacy → Delete My Analytics Data.

What analytics data looks like

  • Aggregated feature-use counts (e.g. "learning mode was started")
  • Bucketed durations and counts (e.g. "session had 5–10 bookmarks")
  • Device OS and app version
  • Your subscription tier (free / pro)

What it never includes

  • Video filenames, paths, or content
  • Bookmark titles, descriptions, or annotations
  • Tag, folder, or playlist names
  • Any free-form text you type
  • Your identity or contact details

Advertising

We do not use PostHog data for targeted advertising, advertising measurement, or to build advertising profiles. We use it solely to improve the product.

Asking first

The app asks once whether you allow anonymous usage data, and sends nothing until you answer. If you choose Don't allow, nothing is sent. Nothing recorded before you answer is sent afterwards.

To change your answer

Open the app, go to Settings → Privacy and turn the Analytics toggle on or off. When it is off, no data is sent and any locally queued events are immediately deleted.

To request deletion of historical data

Go to Settings → Privacy → Copy Analytics ID, then email support@clipento.com with your ID and we will request deletion from PostHog. We will process your request within 30 days.

Cloud sync (optional)

By default, your bookmarks, tags, collections and app data remain only on your device. We never see them.

If you choose to enable cloud sync, your organisational data is securely stored using Google Firebase Firestore. At that point, bookmarks, tags, collections and app data are synced to our servers and can be accessed by Lithe Studios for account recovery, backup, and troubleshooting purposes. Your video files are never uploaded to the cloud — only metadata is synced.

Google Firebase Privacy Policy →

Teaching Packs

If you join a teacher's pack, your teacher can see your display name and email address, when you joined, and your practice activity on the moves in that pack (which moves you practised and for how long). Your own notes on a pack stay private to you.

If you publish a pack, your students can see your display name and the pack's content. Your display name is visible to other signed-in Clipento users.

Leaving a pack, or being removed by the teacher, ends that access. Deleting your account removes your membership, your seat with the teacher and your practice activity.

YouTube videos

Clipento uses YouTube API Services to play YouTube videos you add. They play in YouTube's own embedded player. Clipento never downloads, stores or re-hosts the video itself.

When you add a YouTube video, Clipento stores its link and details such as its title and thumbnail, together with your bookmarks and tags. These stay on your device, and are also stored in your cloud library if you use cloud sync. To get the title and thumbnail, the app asks YouTube for them. Clipento does not access your YouTube or Google account.

When a YouTube video plays, YouTube (a Google service) receives information from your device, such as your IP address and which video is playing, and may store cookies or similar identifiers on your device. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, not by us.

By watching YouTube videos in Clipento you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. You can remove a YouTube video and its bookmarks from Clipento at any time; this deletes what Clipento stored about it, including from your cloud library.

Accounts & authentication

If you sign in, to use cloud sync or to open a Teaching Pack, we create a user account using your email address. We use Google Firebase Auth, which assigns a unique user ID to authenticate your account and securely link your bookmarks across devices. This ID is used only for account management, security, and device sync — it is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.

In-app purchases & subscriptions

Clipento offers optional Pro subscription upgrades processed through Apple App Store and Google Play. We do not handle or store your payment information. All payments are processed by Apple or Google directly, and we never see your payment card or account details.

Subscription management and cancellation is handled through your App Store or Google Play account settings.

  • YouTube (Google) — Playback of YouTube videos you add, in YouTube's embedded player (see YouTube videos)

Data retention

Local data: Data stored on your device remains there until you delete the app or manually clear your data through Settings.

Account and cloud data: Retained until you delete your account, which you can do in the app under Settings. You can also email support@clipento.com and we will process your request within 30 days. If you are a teacher with active students, your packs stay available to them for 30 days after you delete your account and are then removed.

Crash reports: Crash reports sent to Google Crashlytics are retained on Google's servers for up to 90 days before automatic deletion. You can delete your local crash logs by clearing app data from your device settings. To request expedited deletion, contact support@clipento.com and we will request it from Google.

Analytics data: PostHog retains pseudonymous analytics data according to their privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Clipento is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact support@clipento.com and we will delete it.

Your rights (UK & GDPR)

If you are located in the United Kingdom or European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, delete, and port your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@clipento.com.

2. What Clipento is

Clipento is a mobile application for bookmarking, annotating, and organising video content for learning purposes. It is designed for dancers, martial artists, and movement learners to mark key moments in video recordings and build a structured practice library.

The App operates primarily on your device. You can add videos from your device's photo library or by providing a remote URL. Videos from your photo library stay on your device — Clipento does not upload them. Remote URLs are accessed directly from the source and are not re-hosted by Clipento.

  • May be included in .clipento export packages to enable cross-device bookmark alignment.
  • The extracted audio data is temporary and deleted after analysis.
