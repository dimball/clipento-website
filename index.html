Sound familiar?

Every dancer who learns from video hits these. Here's how Clipento gets you past each one, in about twenty seconds.

Mark it. Find it. Own it.

The whole loop, from the video you took after class to the move you can do without thinking. All of it free.

01 · MARK

Bookmark the exact moment

Tap as you watch to mark a move. Name it, tag it, and it's one tap away from then on.

02 · FIND

Find any move instantly

Search every video by move name, tag or date and jump straight to the moment you need.

03 · PRACTISE

From watching to doing

Loop a move, slow it down, mirror the video, and mark how it's going.

PRO

Watch your moves become vocabulary.

Vocabulary keeps the moves you've learnt fresh, so a move from three months ago is still yours when the song comes on.

YouTube classes too.

Found a class on YouTube? Share it straight to Clipento. Bookmark the move, tag it, and it sits next to your own clips in search. Turn on loop, slow it down, and drill it until it's yours.

Videos play in YouTube's own player: Clipento keeps your bookmarks and tags, never the video.

PRO

Every workshop, filled by the timetable.

Take a photo of a festival or workshop timetable and the programme scanner turns it into classes, ready for your videos.

FOR TEACHERS

Share your moves with your class.

Publish a pack of the class's moves. Students join with an invite and get every move, and every update, in their own library.
